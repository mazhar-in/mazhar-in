Theory & Mechanics

Concept & Formula Breakdown

This micro-tool computes the exact kinematic and dynamic evolution of the system using numerical integration. In a conservative field, the mechanical energy remains invariant:

$$E = K + U = \frac{1}{2} m v^2 + U(\vec{r}) = \text{constant}$$

Equations of Motion

The governing differential equations are solved at each discrete frame time $\Delta t$:

$$\frac{d^2\vec{r}}{dt^2} = \frac{\vec{F}_{\text{net}}(\vec{r}, \vec{v})}{m}$$

Key Pedagogical Insights

  • Observe how velocity vectors and acceleration decouple during non-linear trajectories.
  • Notice the phase-space conservation under conservative initial boundaries.
