Welcome to **mazhar.in**. This post marks the official beginning of a dedicated space to document my work, ideas, and reflections.

Over the years, I have set up personal blogs multiple times with the intention of writing consistently. Inevitably, the unrelenting demands of training competitive physics students for JEE Advanced, building educational businesses, and mentoring kept pulling me back into the field. 

Most of my thoughts ended up scribbled across dozens of physical paper notepads—derivations of tricky mechanics problems, observations on the challenges facing educators, practical advice for students, and business insights.

## The Need for Human Dialogue in an Era of AI

In an era increasingly dominated by synthetic content and automated feeds, the urge to write authentically has never been stronger. While AI is a powerful leverage tool, it often lacks personal soul—the grit of real classroom experience, the hard-earned lessons of entrepreneurship, and genuine human connection.

This publication space exists to bridge that gap.

## What You Can Expect Here

Moving forward, I will be publishing essays and technical breakdowns across a few core domains:

1. **Analytical Physics**: Deconstructing high-order problems in classical mechanics, electrodynamics, and modern physics—distilling complex multi-concept challenges into intuitive, calculus-driven solutions.
2. **Instructional Engineering & Tooling**: Architectural notes on the software systems I build—from testing engines and canvas simulators to procedural exam automation at [SMA Physics](https://smaphysics.com).
3. **Entrepreneurial Lessons & Life Notes**: Honest reflections from building educational ventures, mentoring students, and navigating the ever-shifting intersection of technology and education.

If you’ve landed here, thank you for reading. I welcome your thoughts, feedback, and questions as we embark on this dialogue.
