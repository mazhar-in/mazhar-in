A live benchmark and sandbox validating client-side KaTeX rendering across inline typography, algebraic equations, and complex physics notation.

## 1. Inline Fractions & Algebraic Radicals

Inline mathematics must integrate smoothly with body copy without pushing line heights or causing jarring vertical jitter.

- Simple rational fraction: $\frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}$
- The quadratic discriminant: $\Delta = \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}$
- Roots of a quadratic polynomial:
  $$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

## 2. Fundamental Quantum Dynamics

The time-dependent Schrödinger equation governing the evolution of a quantum state vector $|\Psi(t)\rangle$ in Hilbert space:

$$i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t} |\Psi(t)\rangle = \hat{H} |\Psi(t)\rangle$$

Where:
- $\hbar$ is the reduced Planck constant ($\frac{h}{2\pi}$)
- $\hat{H}$ is the Hamiltonian operator representing the total energy of the physical system
- $|\Psi(t)\rangle$ is the state ket vector

All equations scale sharply across dark and light modes with sub-millisecond execution.
